### Off-course maneuvering

`off-course-maneuvering` — briefed by N4202, T3301, T4201

> No publication in the reference set defines or describes off-course maneuvering. The LAT FTI (CNATRA P-912), now in the set, was checked and has no section on it; its only related lines are §108 item 8 ("Do not attempt to re-enter a MTR after leaving the route structure", p. 1-19) and the 45 degree/parallel ridgeline crossing "in cases where your navigation will allow deviation" (§107, p. 1-11). The low-level FTIs have only: do not change heading or airspeed until an exact point of reference is determined, and if the checkpoint has not been identified, turn on time (C-130 Low-Level FTI §105, Tilt-Rotor Low-Level FTI §405); for loss of SA, climb to the MSA (same sections, item 8a); and the optional route width annotation (C-130 §103). Ask the squadron what the item expects before compiling from these.

### Scatter plan

`scatter-plan` — briefed by T4201

> No publication in the reference set defines or describes a scatter plan. The LAT FTI (CNATRA P-912) was checked and does not mention one (its KIO list in §108, p. 1-19, includes "Two or more aircraft lose sight", which is not a scatter plan). A squadron tactical briefing guide is the likely source.
